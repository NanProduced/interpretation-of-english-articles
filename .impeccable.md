## Design Context

### Users
English language learners using the Claread application for reading, annotation, and dictionary lookups. They need to report issues with content accuracy (parsing, translations, phonetics) or general app experience without having their reading flow disrupted.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, reading-first, unobtrusive. It acts as a "silent quality loop" rather than a loud customer service portal. 

### Aesthetic Direction
"Paper-like" (纸感) reading system. 
- Uses warm paper colors (`--reader-paper`) instead of stark white cards.
- Restrained and elegant, avoiding generic dashboard aesthetics, emoji icons, or heavy overlays.
- Minimalist entries in the reading flow, expanding to stable, full-width bottom sheets or dedicated pages only when needed.

### Design Principles
1. **Light Entry, Stable Form (入口轻，表单稳)**: Minimal disruption during reading; clear and stable forms upon interaction.
2. **Structure Over Free-text (分类先于长文本)**: Prioritize structured issue selection over making users type long descriptions.
3. **Automated Context (上下文自动采集)**: Never make the user repeat what the system already knows (article, word, annotation context).
4. **Visible Resolution (状态可见，闭环反馈)**: Feedback is not a black box; users must see states (received, adopted, resolved) and rewards.
5. **Separate Positive/Negative Paths (正负反馈分开)**: "Helpful" is a one-click action; reporting issues opens the detailed form.
